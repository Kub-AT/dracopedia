# Contributing to the archive

Useful contributions include historical captures of missing pages, broken-link reports, evidence of original licensing terms, attribution corrections, and fixes to the archive's helper interface.

For a content correction, supply an original Dracopedia source or an archived capture. Do not invent missing article text, rewrite historical articles as current game advice, or silently replace them with modern content. Clearly distinguish a recovered historical source from an editorial note.

For an asset contribution, document its source and any known rights or license. Do not assume that an image found online is reusable. Preserve existing author and license notices.

Before submitting changes to `docs/`, check that internal article links and asset paths remain relative and resolve to existing files. Keep helper-page styling scoped to helper pages; original articles should retain their recovered presentation where possible. Do not commit response caches, local databases, credentials, or machine logs.

Open an issue before proposing broad changes to recovery policy or licensing. See [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md) for attribution and removal requests.
